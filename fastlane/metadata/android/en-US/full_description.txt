Open and edit your KeePass vault on your phone. Changes save to the same file you open in KeePass 2.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Opens KDBX 4.0 and 4.1 vaults</li>
<li>Unlocks with a password, key file, fingerprint or face</li>
<li>Searches entries and copies fields; the clipboard clears after 30 seconds</li>
<li>Adds, edits and deletes entries, with history and recycle bin</li>
<li>Locks when the screen turns off or 1 minute after you leave the app</li>
</ul>

<b>Saving</b>
<ul>
<li>Saves to the same file</li>
<li>Checks for newer changes before saving</li>
<li>Reads each save back to check it</li>
<li>Keeps encrypted backups on the phone</li>
</ul>

<b>Security</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission, no accounts, no tracking</li>
<li>Never writes decrypted data to storage or logs</li>
<li>Blocks screenshots and other apps' overlays</li>
<li>Refuses unsafe or weakened unlock settings</li>
</ul>

<b>Not included</b>
Autofill, sync servers, creating vaults, KDBX 3, opening attachments, TOTP codes.

Requires Android 12 or later and a KDBX 4 vault.

Free and open source (AGPL). Not affiliated with KeePass.
